WLKATA MT4 Robotic Arm CAD Model

The robotic arm geometry used in the ARIA-4 simulation is based on the WLKATA MT4 CAD model.

Original creator: WLKATA Robotics

Source: https://github.com/wlkata/MT4-STL

License: Apache License 2.0

The ARIA-4 project does not claim original authorship of the WLKATA MT4 mechanical geometry.

The model is used as a robotic platform for the development and experimental evaluation of the ARIA-4 software system, including LLM-based command processing, vision-based localization, kinematic control, and workspace safety validation.

Any modifications to the original model should be documented here before publication.
